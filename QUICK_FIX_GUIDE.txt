🔒 WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Your MongoDB had:
  Student: loginid: "ST001"  password: "123456"         ← PLAIN TEXT ❌
  Student: loginid: "ST002"  password: "password"       ← PLAIN TEXT ❌
  Faculty: loginid: "FC001"  password: "faculty123"     ← PLAIN TEXT ❌

After migration it becomes:
  Student: loginid: "ST001"  password: "$2b$10$h8..." ← HASHED ✅
  Student: loginid: "ST002"  password: "$2b$10$k9..." ← HASHED ✅
  Faculty: loginid: "FC001"  password: "$2b$10$m7..." ← HASHED ✅

But login still works with ORIGINAL PASSWORD because:
  1. You enter: "123456"
  2. We hash it: bcrypt.hash("123456") → matches database hash
  3. Login successful! ✅
